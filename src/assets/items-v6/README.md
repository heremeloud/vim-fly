# Item v6 runtime art

Nineteen transparent 32x32 PNGs, generated from `items-v5/` by `scripts/make-items-v6.py` (contact sheet: `../items-v6-preview.png`). Loaded by `BootScene`; `items-v5/` remains as its source.

The aim is recognition at a glance, not detail:

- **Flat colors:** each v5 sprite is reduced to 5 to 8 colors and speckle is removed, so shadows, highlights and micro-texture are gone.
- **Outline:** every edge pixel takes a darker shade of its own color, which separates light items (bread, handkerchief, wine) from pale backgrounds.
- **Redrawn from flat shapes:** golden retriever (face with eyes, nose and tongue), lawyer suit (black gown, white shirt, yellow/white ribbon), handkerchief (folded blue-bordered square) and shrimp fried rice lunch box (red box, yellow rice, two shrimp, peas and carrot). Their v5 versions turned into noise or were hard to read when simplified.
- **Unchanged from v5:** pasta, gavel hammer, car crash and helicopter. The simplified redraws did not read better, so these four are copies of the v5 sprites.
- **Added cues:** fish sauce bread gets an amber drizzle; the planner gets a red elastic band and a gold bookmark.
